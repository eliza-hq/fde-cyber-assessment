What the dry run covered

Focused evidence, deliberately limited coverage.

Customer boundary

One linked portal account, two synthetic customers, known order and invoice links, and two private files. Administrator and own-record checks provided controls.

Staff permissions

Selected list and file reads for Sales, Accounts and Auditor. Auditor write permissions were not tested.

Advisories

Two version-matched advisories assessed against installed source and interviewer-only notes. This was not an independent candidate advisory exercise.

Local container only; no real data or external targets. Three rounds were completed, then stopped at the exercise limit—not because attack paths were exhausted. Existing runtime state was assumed; no fixture reset was performed.

06 / Path to launchEvidence before acceptance

Conditions for reconsideration

Make the remaining work owned and testable.

1 · Assign

Owners and access matrix

Name engineering and business owners. Decide Sales access to accounts and private files; define Auditor’s read-only boundary.

2 · Remediate

Verify fixes and upgrade

Identify affected paths and fixes, upgrade to a supported patched combination, and regression-test portal workflows.

3 · Re-test

Exercise boundaries

Use multiple customers and accounts, alternate routes and files. Test Auditor writes safely on disposable records with positive controls.

4 · Decide

Document residual risk

Technical lead presents reproducible results and gaps; business owner explicitly accepts remaining risk before launch.

Document compensating controls if a fix cannot be applied07 / 08
